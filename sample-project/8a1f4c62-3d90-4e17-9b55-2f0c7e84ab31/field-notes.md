# Field notes

Loose observations, not yet worth a document of their own.

Every tool that promises to organise your thinking ends up organising your
files instead. The two are not the same job, and the second one is much
easier to sell.

People describe their notes as a garden and then treat them as an attic.

The useful unit is rarely the note. It is the sentence you can find again
six months later, in a note you had forgotten you wrote.
