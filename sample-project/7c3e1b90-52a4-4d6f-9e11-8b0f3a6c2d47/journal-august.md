# Hut journal — August 1912

**3 August.** Twilight at noon thirty-one minutes and lengthening by rather more than three minutes a day, which is faster than my table said and I have checked the table twice. I have put the daily figure on the wall by the door. It is read more than anything else I have ever recorded. — E.P.B.

**6 August.** Light enough now at midday to work outside without a lamp for the first time since April, and I have got more done in two hours than in any week of July. I did not know how much of the winter was the dark itself until it started going. — J.L.

**8 August.** Sledges out of the annexe and onto the bench. Runners want doing, three of the cases want new lids, and the tent Thorsen says is sound is not sound, and he and I have looked at the same seam and seen different things. I will mend it my way and not tell him. — J.L.

**12 August.** Sugar fifty-six. Depot list still says what it said in July. I have now put both figures on the same sheet, the one above the other, because I have twice been told the sum is fine by men who had not seen the two numbers together. It is not fine. It is possible, which is not the same. — H.C.

**16 August.** Light enough at noon to see the ridge line, and everything has changed. Men who did not want to talk in July cannot be got to stop. Two arguments today, both trivial, both about tools, and neither was about tools. This is the part of a winter nobody warns you about — the dark was quiet and the coming out is not. — W.S.

**18 August.** Two days out from the sun coming back and the whole hut has become a calendar. Barrow's figure by the door, the depot list by the annexe, Culshaw's sugar on the door under it. A man cannot cross the room without being told how much of something is left. — W.S.

**20 August.** Sun. Four minutes of it, and Barrow had the whole hut out with the figures worked in advance so that nobody would miss it. Thorsen shook hands again. Leggat, who has not been warm since May, stood in it with his eyes shut. — G.R.M.

**24 August.** Now we can move, everything wants deciding at once and Mowbray decides in the order things are put in front of him, which is not the order they matter in. I have said so. Thorsen has said so, differently, and was listened to, which I have noticed and will not mention again. — H.C.

**27 August.** Barometer 28.71, wind NE force 7, drift heavy — the first real blow since the light came back and it took the tent off the bench and thirty yards down the slope. Recovered. The seam I mended held and the seam Thorsen was content with did not, and I have not said anything about that and do not intend to. — J.L.

**31 August.** Dogs are another animal since the light. The lame one is sound. I have them out four hours a day now and they would go eight. The men are the same and I do not think the men are ready. — A.T.
