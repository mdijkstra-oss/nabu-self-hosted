# Private notebook — G. R. Mowbray

Not the hut journal. Kept for myself, in the small book, and not left on the table.

**14 March.** Culshaw resents me and I set him to the stores on the first afternoon knowing he would. It was the right order and I gave it badly, in front of Leggat, and I have been paying interest on that for a week.

**19 April.** Sparrow asked after Leggat's hand and I let him think I had noticed it myself. I had not. Thorsen told me.

**22 April.** Culshaw is right about the sugar and I have not said so, because if I say so in April I have nothing left to say in August. This is a poor reason and it is the reason.

**11 May.** Asking each man separately what the depot should carry is a trick and they will work it out. Thorsen already has; he gave me an answer for a room rather than for me, and then a second answer at the dogs an hour later, which was the real one.

**19 June.** The speech was too long and I stopped it early and everyone was kind about it. Barrow's menus are the best thing anyone has done here. He would be mortified to be told so, so I have not told him, which is cowardice dressed as tact.

**11 July.** Thorsen says the plan will not survive the surface. He is right. I am planning it anyway because the planning is holding four men together through the darkest part of the year, and the plan being wrong is a cost I am prepared to carry. I have not said this to anyone and will not.

**19 July.** Culshaw has put the sugar figure on the annexe door. It is a reproach and it is meant to be read as one and he is entitled to it.

**6 September.** I named the depot party before I told Culshaw what it takes out of his stores, and I did that because I did not want the argument to change the party. So the argument happened afterwards and was worse and was about the same thing. I knew that would happen. I have written down that I knew, so that I cannot later tell myself I did not.

**17 September.** The dog feed argument is not about dog feed. Thorsen wants the depot further out because he is the only man here who can get it back from there. Leggat wants it nearer because he does not trust the surface and does not want to say he does not trust Thorsen. I could end it in one sentence and I am letting it run, because when it ends they will have ended it, and I need them to have done that before we are sixty miles out.

**5 October.** They went at eight. I have not written anything about how that felt because I do not have it yet.

**31 October.** Sparrow's light in the north window is pointless and I have not said so and will not. There is a difference between a thing that is useless and a thing that is doing something other than what it says it is doing, and I have spent this winter learning it.
