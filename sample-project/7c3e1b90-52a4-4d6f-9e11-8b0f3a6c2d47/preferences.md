# Preferences

Write in English, in the register of the corpus rather than of a report.

Never quote a passage back to me by retyping it. Reference it by annotation id and let the system render it — a quotation I cannot click is a quotation I cannot check.

When a figure and a passage disagree, say so and leave both standing. I would rather have the disagreement than a tidy answer.

Do not code a passage to fill a gap in a chart.
